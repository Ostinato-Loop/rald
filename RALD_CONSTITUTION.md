## MISSION

RALD exists to help people build trusted identities,
meaningful relationships, strong communities,
and economic opportunities through technology.

**RALD is African-first and globally scalable.**

---

## CORE PRINCIPLES

### 1. Identity First

> One User. One Identity. Many Products.

- **Profiles owns identity.** Products consume identity.
- No product may create a parallel user database.
- Authentication must be centralized at `profiles.rald.cloud`.
- Every user's RALD ID must be portable across all products.

### 2. Relationship First

> Relationships are the primary asset. Not page views. Not downloads. Not impressions.

- Every product must strengthen relationships between real people.
- Discovery features must connect users who share real context (rooms, communities, events).
- The relationship graph is a shared platform asset — not owned by any single product.

### 3. Community First

> Communities are more valuable than audiences.

RALD prioritizes:
- Local communities (city, neighborhood)
- Campus communities (universities, schools)
- Creator communities (fans, collaborators)
- Professional communities (industry, trade)

An audience watches. A community acts.

### 4. Voice First

> Voice is the primary communication medium. Text is secondary.

- Products must support natural spoken communication.
- Audio rooms are a core Loop feature — not an afterthought.
- Voice interfaces must be designed for all literacy levels.

### 5. African First

Design for:
- Low bandwidth (2G/3G must be usable)
- Mobile-only users (no desktop assumption)
- Local languages (8+ African languages minimum)
- Regional cultures (content must be regionally relevant)
- Emerging markets (data cost is real money)

If a feature cannot run on a ₦3,000 Android phone on 3G in Lagos, it is not ready.

### 6. Trust Before Growth

> User trust is more important than growth metrics.

RALD will not:
- Use dark patterns
- Farm engagement through outrage
- Manipulate users psychologically
- Sell private conversation data
- Prioritize growth over user wellbeing

## IDENTITY RULES (Non-negotiable)

1. **Profiles is the sole identity provider.** No exceptions.
2. **Loop must not own identity.** Loop consumes identity from Profiles.
3. **Messenger must not own identity.** Messenger consumes identity from Profiles.
4. **Future products must not own identity.** This rule applies permanently.
5. **Users authenticate once.** One login → access to all products.
6. **Authentication must work across all products.** SSO is not optional.
7. **RALD JWT (HS256)** is the cross-app token format. `RALD_JWT_SECRET` is the shared secret.
8. **Logout must propagate.** Signing out of one product must offer to sign out everywhere.

## AI RULES

RALD AI may learn from:
- Public content
- Public communities
- Public creator content
- Explicit user opt-in contributions

RALD AI must **never** learn from:
- Private messages
- Private conversations
- Non-consenting users
- Content shared in private communities

**Violation of these AI rules is grounds for immediate removal of any team member.**
